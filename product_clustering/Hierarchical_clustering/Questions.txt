2. Sub-clusters That Are Identical to Their Parent Cluster (No Real Granularity)

Original prompt (condensed):

“Something seems wrong with our clustering mechanism b/c these sub-clusters are just equal to the bigger cluster.”

Improved prompt:

Context
Expected hierarchy:
broad_categories_XXX → broad_specific_products_YY → broad_variants_ZZ

Dataset Rows Exhibiting the Issue

broad_categories_116 | broad_specific_products_42 | broad_variants_8 | 250682  | hot dog sinai ab skl6-1 2/5 pound 064
broad_categories_116 | broad_specific_products_42 | broad_variants_8 | 250682  | hot dog sinai ab skl6-1 2/5 pound 064-z
broad_categories_116 | broad_specific_products_42 | broad_variants_8 | 22095N3 | hot dog sinai ab skl8-1 2/5 pound 065-z

Notice that broad_variants_8 contains all SKUs that are already together in broad_specific_products_42, providing no additional separation.

Your Task
	1.	Detect whether the variant-level clustering threshold is too permissive or if a tie-breaker step is missing.
	2.	Determine if extra features—e.g., size codes (064, 065), trailing “-z” modifiers, pack sizes (“2/5 pound”)—should trigger variant splits.
	3.	Recommend a revised rule set or algorithmic tweak so that broad_variants delivers finer granularity (e.g., broad_variants_8a, 8b, 8c).
	4.	Return your diagnosis and a bullet-point remediation plan; include pseudo-code if algorithm changes are involved.
