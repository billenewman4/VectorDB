### Price and Cost Analysis

- **Price Range:** $5.99 to $93.95 (avg. $35.41)
- **Cost Range:** $5.90 to $91.00 (avg. $34.48)

### Root Cause Analysis

**The primary driver of margin inconsistency is pricing.**

Price variation is significantly higher than cost variation. Products in this cluster have similar costs but are being sold at different price points, resulting in inconsistent margins.

### Recommendations

1. **Standardize pricing strategy** for products in this cluster.
2. Review sales data to understand if higher-priced items in this cluster sell at comparable volumes.
3. Consider implementing pricing guidelines for sales teams to ensure consistency.
4. Evaluate if the higher-margin products can inform pricing strategy for the lower-margin ones.
